About

A hop-on-hop-off tour, minus the fee.

Every city we cover already runs a public bus network built for its own residents. In quite a few of them, that network happens to pass most of the same landmarks a paid sightseeing bus would show you — for the price of one regular ticket instead of twenty-five or thirty euros a day.

We didn't assume that's true everywhere. Each city on this site was checked against its own public transit data before being added: which sights sit within an easy walk of a bus stop, which bus lines actually connect enough of them, and whether that bus runs often enough to be useful rather than theoretical. Cities where the historic centre is small enough to just walk, or where the sights are really a tram or metro ride away, aren't included — the whole point is a bus that earns its place.

How a route gets picked

  • Sights come from OpenStreetMap, cross-checked against real transit stops.
  • Routes come from each city's own open transit data (GTFS).
  • A line only qualifies if it runs at least every 20–30 minutes.
  • We rank routes on how many sights they reach and how spread out those sights are — a line touching five sights on one street loses to one touching four sights across four different neighbourhoods.

What this isn't

Not a replacement for a transit app, and not a guarantee of the fastest way anywhere. Walking distances shown are straight-line, not routed — always check the operator's own site for live times.
