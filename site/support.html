Support

Common questions, answered plainly.

A route sent me somewhere with no sight nearby

Walking distances on the map are straight-line, not routed — a river, a highway, or a hill can turn a "250 m" walk into a longer one. Let us know which stop and city and we'll take a look.

The bus times don't match what I saw in the app

We show typical service frequency, not a live timetable. Always check the local operator's own site or app before you travel — a link is on every city page.

My city isn't listed, or says "coming soon"

We only add a city once its own transit data shows a bus route that genuinely earns a spot — reaches enough sights, spread across enough of the city, running often enough to be useful. Some cities we looked at didn't clear that bar yet; others are still being built out. See About for how that decision gets made.
